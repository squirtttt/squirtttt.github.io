I am Youna Jang, and I received my M.S. degree in Artificial Intelligence Applications from Kwangwoon University, South Korea. During my M.S. studies, I conducted research as a member of the [AIaaS Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/aiaas) under the supervision of Prof. Sangmin Lee. 

My research interests lie at the intersection of **Neural Architecture Search (NAS)** and **Medical Image Analysis** and **efficient and trustworthy AI**.

When I'm not doing research, you'll probably find me 🏊‍♀️swimming, 📚reading, and 🎧listening to music.
